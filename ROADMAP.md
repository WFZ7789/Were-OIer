# Were-OIer Roadmap

## v0.3 — Rebuild the game loop

- One cross-platform C++20/CMake codebase.
- Deterministic game state and event log.
- Stable five-role night actions and victory rules.
- Structured discussion actions that change beliefs and votes.
- Explainable AI voting and regression coverage for legacy hangs/crashes.

## v0.4 — Deepen deduction

- Per-agent memory of claims, contradictions and voting history.
- Distinct AI personalities and intentional wolf deception.
- Hybrid player discussion: structured intent plus optional free text.
- Replay/save format with versioned compatibility.

## v0.5 — Restore and expand identity

- Reintroduce Magician and White Wolf King on the new action/event model.
- Scenario and role-deck construction before a match.
- Rebuild endings and achievements around meaningful play patterns.
- Replace the old role shop with non-pay-to-win unlocks or challenge modifiers.
